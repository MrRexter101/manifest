## Scope

Manifest live companion: phone app (index.html, Operate), projector (screen.html, Experience for the room), host console (host.html, Operate). The Canva deck is out of scope for this round.

## Audience and task

Logistics staff in a bright Pattaya hotel room, mostly on phones, English as a second language; one trainer at a laptop. Task: join fast, answer live questions, run labs, save prompts, leave with a plan.

## Constraints

Static, no build. Phone-first 375 px. Projector legible from the back of a bright room. Shapes plus colour on answer tiles. Every JS hook and flow keeps working; the 18-step E2E must pass. Class runs 14 Oct 2026, so no risky markup rewrites.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An Apple-made app that lives in one place: Laem Chabang at first light, lit by one low sun above-left. The room's job always reads first. It refuses flat white SaaS, Kahoot candy and the dark neon "AI" look.

OWN-WORLD: Atmosphere far to near: sea-haze blue #7FA6CC, morning air #BFD3E6, haze #E6EEF4, sand horizon #F7EBDD; the ground is the near stop. Three materials, each lit / body / shade: frosted glass chrome with a lit top lip and a down-right cast; painted container steel for anything you tap or earn (safety orange #FF6A00 primary, locked steel desaturated); label paper for the participant's own work (white stock, black rule, mono data, barcode, fine grain). SF Pro type, iOS large titles, tabular numerals. Dark mode is the same port re-lit at night.

STORY: join in ten seconds; "Good to see you, Nok." names the one next thing; the route shows how far the shipment has come; rank and XP grow with the day; leave with a label-printed Manifest.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone join. Dawn sky over a container-stack horizon, "Manifest." as an iOS large title, course name one line, the route Laem Chabang to Pattaya with one live orange node, then a frosted join sheet: name, table chips as small container swatches, one orange steel pill "Come aboard".

FORM: User-pinned in their own words: Apple-made UI plus their SkyPath and Meridian signature (place with a lighting model, modelled materials, route map, rank and XP, greeting home). Choices confirmed 13 Oct 2026: Port at first light, SF Pro, all four pieces, all three surfaces. Canon path, no concept roll; seed key: none (user-pinned).
